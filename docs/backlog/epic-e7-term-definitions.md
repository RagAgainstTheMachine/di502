## S1 · Ask what an AI term means

**Acceptance criteria**

- When the user asks what a term means and the glossary has an entry for it, the assistant shall give that entry's definition.
- When the user asks about a term by its English name, the assistant shall give the glossary entry with that English name.
- If the glossary has no entry for the term, then the assistant shall not present a definition as coming from the glossary.
